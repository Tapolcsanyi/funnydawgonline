About us

Helping people. Providing energy.

Vantex Materials Solutions exists to connect human need with dependable supply. We believe access to energy and essential materials is foundational to dignity, health, and opportunity—whether in a growing city, a remote community, or an industrial corridor undertaking its next transition.

Our teams work with regulators, local stakeholders, and technical partners to deliver projects that are safe, accountable, and built for the long term. We invest in people first: training, safety culture, and clear communication across every stage of a program’s life.

Corporate headquarters located in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, where materials research, pilot systems, and field integration share one secured campus.

Leadership

Vantex was founded by a small group of scientists and operators who believed industrial-scale energy could be delivered without losing sight of the communities it serves.

Maria Vance

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

A native of rural East Tennessee, Maria Vance brings deep technical expertise to Vantex, holding dual doctorates in materials engineering and applied energy systems earned concurrently.

After establishing the company, she strategically headquartered Vantex in her hometown to foster regional economic development and innovation. Under her leadership, Vantex continues to thrive alongside the community she is dedicated to serving.

Daniel Mercer

Co-founder & Chief Operations Officer

A seasoned operational leader with a background in plant management and logistics, Daniel engineered the field deployment playbook that powers Vantex’s project execution. He maintains enterprise-wide oversight of contractor safety performance, ensuring rigorous operational standards across all sites.

Daniel R. Whitford

Co-founder & Chief Information Officer

Leads Vantex’s enterprise IT strategy and infrastructure. Whitford drives technological alignment to support scalable business growth. Beyond his core IT leadership, he directs operations at the Oak Ridge pilot line and plays a key role in developing emerging talent by mentoring Vantex’s university fellowship cohort.

Julie Stephens

Co-founder & General Counsel

Directs regulatory affairs and permitting strategy for Vantex’s domestic operations. She spearheaded the negotiation of Vantex’s landmark first interstate materials corridor agreement, establishing a critical framework for regional expansion.
